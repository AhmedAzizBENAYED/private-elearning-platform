Tracking logic

How learning activity is recorded and how progress, status and “active now” are computed.

Principles

What the admin screens promise, and what the data must guarantee.

Opening is not completing

A formation, module or lesson that was only opened stays In progress. Only a recorded lesson completion moves the percentage.

Progress is derived

The percentage is never typed in or stored by the screen. It is computed from completed video lessons ÷ all video lessons.

One last-activity date

Every learning event updates a single “last activity” timestamp per member and per course. Sorting and “Recently active” read it.

Honest presence

Active now is an estimate from events received in the last 5 minutes. Without it the UI shows the most recently viewed lesson.

Rules

Calculation rules

Lesson done
A VIDEO lesson has a completed-at time. Text, document and link lessons never count.
Progress %
round( completed VIDEO lessons ÷ total VIDEO lessons × 100 ). 5 of 11 gives 45%.
Module done
All VIDEO lessons of the module are done. Shown as “3 / 5 modules”.
Not started
No course-opened event. Shown with a dashed bar and 0%.
In progress
Opened, but not every VIDEO lesson is completed. 0% is possible.
Completed
Every VIDEO lesson is completed. Keeps its completion date.
Last activity
Latest event time of the member (overall) or of the member in that course.
Active now
Last event or “still on the lesson” signal is 5 minutes old or less. Threshold TO CONFIRM.
