Rules

Do
Use size and weight to create contrast and hierarchy.
Keep body text ≥ 16 px and captions ≥ 12 px.
Limit reading lines to about 72 characters.
Less is more: one H1 per page, few weights per screen.
Don’t
Scale text horizontally or vertically.
Add strokes, outlines or drop shadows to text.
Use the light / thin weights for text (300 and below).
Put brand-blue small text on white (3.30 : 1).
