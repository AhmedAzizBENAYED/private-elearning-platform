Charte analysis

What the JEENISo identity manual (March 2018, ed. 1) defines, and how it becomes an application UI.

From the manual to the interface

Topic
What the charte says
What the platform does
Logo
Three versions: colour, black (#131313) and reverse. Minimum 30 mm. Protective space X on all sides. Forbidden: recolouring, adding elements, frames, white boxes on busy photos, drop shadows, skew, non-proportional scaling, screening. Logo always on the left on stationery and e-mail.
Colour logo on white (headers, login form), reverse on navy (admin sidebar, login photo). Header logo 120 px wide (min 113). Logo top-left everywhere. Extracted from the manual; swap for approved vector files.
Primary colours
Blue #3594D6 (Pantone 2925 C) and gray #615F5F (Cool Gray 10 C) — the two colours of the signature.
Blue = brand accent (rules, progress, current state, numerals ≥ 24 px). Gray = secondary text (6.4 : 1). Both kept exact.
Secondary colours
Navy #1A4A6B, blue #71B4E2, charcoal #3A3939, light gray #908F8F. Tone-on-tone combinations of neighbours on the colour wheel.
Navy = actions, links, selected states (9.4 : 1, so AA where blue is not). Charcoal = body text and avatars. Light blue = tints and dark-surface accents. Light gray = decorative rules and control borders.
Colour with photography
Pick the palette colour closest to the dominant colour of the photo; overlay a translucent band; text must contrast.
Course thumbnails are duotone versions of the manual’s architectural photos. Login and completion use a 93 % navy band with white text.
Typography
Montserrat (Thin → Black) for everything; Arial for body copy only when Montserrat is not suitable. No scaling, strokes or shadows. “Less is more”.
Montserrat 400–900 in a 13-step scale (12 px minimum). Weight 300 and below never used for text. Arial is the fallback stack.
Graphic devices
Page header: blue block + gray bar. Titles underlined by a blue rule and a gray rule. Large blue Montserrat numerals (1.0, 2.1…). Cover: monochrome architecture with a blue band and white bold title.
Brand strip (8 px), title rule (4 px), big numerals for modules and completion, photo + navy band. Used sparingly and always in the same places.
Composition
Airy pages, left-aligned text, strong hierarchy through size and weight, rectangular geometry.
Angular layout: 4–6 px radii, 1 px borders, no gradients, no decorative illustration, generous spacing.
Contrast
DO use colours with adequate contrast; DON’T use type without adequate contrast to its background. (The manual itself sets white on light gray and light blue.)
Every pairing audited (see 02 Colors). Where the charte’s literal use fails AA — e.g. white on #3594D6 = 3.30 : 1 — the design uses navy instead and keeps blue for large or non-text elements.

Not defined by the charte — neutral choices made

Error / destructive colour
One sober red (#B3261E) with icon + text. The only extension hue.
UI surfaces
White cards on a cool near-white (#F5F6F7) derived from the palette.
Iconography
Neutral 24 px line icons, 2 px stroke, inherit text colour.
States & spacing
4 px spacing base, focus ring in brand blue, skeletons in gray-100.
